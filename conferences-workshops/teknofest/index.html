Expanded account

The competition where the story started.

TEKNOFEST Sağlıkta Yapay Zekâ (Health in Artificial Intelligence) is the point Ethosoft names as the real beginning of its story. The team was in the period of first learning machine learning and deep learning, and TEKNOFEST was the venue chosen to test that learning on a real problem instead of a classroom exercise.

The team finished third in Türkiye. More than the placement itself, this competition set the pattern the rest of Ethosoft's competition history would follow: pick a genuine problem, build a working system under a deadline, and put it in front of judges who could challenge it.
