Expanded account

A win, and a turning point in how the team thought about winning.

MindBricks Hackathon added another first-place result to Ethosoft's hackathon record. But this period was also when the team's view of competitions itself started to change: the first question the team asked about a project began shifting away from “does it win?” toward questions about how a result should be evaluated, justified, and documented.

MindBricks is recorded here as much for that shift in perspective as for the result itself.
