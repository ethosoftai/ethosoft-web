Expanded account

Turning an idea into a working system, fast.

Coming up with an idea at a hackathon can be easy. The hard part is turning that idea into a working system inside a tightly limited window of time. DataMedX, organized by İstinye University, tested that skill in its most concentrated form: short timeline, real judging, and a system that had to actually run by the deadline.

Ethosoft's team won the championship. The event became an early proving ground for building under real constraints rather than only designing on paper.
