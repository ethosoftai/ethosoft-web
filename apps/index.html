Ethosoft Apps

Research into products.

Ethosoft Apps brings together the user-facing artificial-intelligence products that grow out of our research and engineering work. We test the methods we develop in research against real problems, and the experience of running a product in the hands of real users feeds back into new research questions.

How Apps relates to Works

A different shelf from our research archive.

Works is our archive of research projects and prototypes, described at the maturity level their project records support. Apps is narrower and more concrete: it lists the products we build for people to actually use, each with its own address, its own users, and its own development stage.
