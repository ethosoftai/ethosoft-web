Profile

Research with a path to practice.

Ahmet co-founded Ethosoft and leads strategy while engineering AI and data systems. His work combines careful evaluation with practical prototypes, with a particular interest in making Turkish-language and small-model research more reproducible.

The supplied CV describes work on NEDOQwen, Foresight, Relation LM, document question answering, biomedical tooling, and autonomous robotics. Project and publication pages on this site provide the primary technical context for those efforts.
